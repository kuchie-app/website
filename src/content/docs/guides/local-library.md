The Local collection keeps everything on your iPhone: library, history, reading progress and downloads. Nothing needs a server or a computer.

## Choose Local

Pick **Local** the first time you open Kuchie. If you're already in Server, switch from **Library** or **More → Collection mode**.

## Import files

Kuchie imports:

- **CBZ** and **ZIP** archives of images, one chapter or volume per file.
- **Folders of images**, read in file-name order.

Pages can be JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP or AVIF. CBR, CB7, PDF and EPUB aren't supported yet; convert them to CBZ first.

1. Put the files somewhere the Files app can see: On My iPhone, iCloud Drive, or a connected drive.
2. In Kuchie's Local library, tap **Import manga** and choose files or a folder.
3. The title appears in your library. Open it and tap a chapter to read.

:::tip
Name pages with leading zeros (`001.jpg`, `002.jpg` …) so they sort in the right order.
:::

## Save chapters from extensions

Titles from [extension stores](/guides/extension-stores/) stream from their sites. To keep a chapter for offline reading, choose **Save to device** on it. Saved chapters stay in the Local profile until you remove them.

## Moving between collections

Local and Server are kept separate, including their downloads. Moving a library from one to the other isn't possible yet.

## Where the files live

Everything stays inside Kuchie's own storage on the iPhone. If you delete the app, the Local library goes with it, so [update](/guides/updating/) by installing over the old version instead.
