- **Theme mode:** light, dark or follow the system.
- **Pure black (AMOLED):** true black backgrounds in dark mode.
- **Liquid Glass:** used automatically on iOS 26.
- **App icon:** choose one of the alternate Home Screen icons. iOS confirms the change with a short message.
- **App language:** Kuchie is translated into 20+ languages. Pick one in the settings, or follow the iPhone's language.

Want to help translate? See [Translate](/translate/).
