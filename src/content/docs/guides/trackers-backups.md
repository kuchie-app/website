These tools work through your Suwayomi server, so they're available in the Server collection.

## Trackers

Open **Trackers**, log in to the tracking sites your server supports, then add a title to a tracker from its page. Progress updates as you read. Removing a tracking entry can also remove it from the tracking site if you choose.

## Backups

- **Create backup** saves your library, categories, history and tracking.
- **Restore backup** brings it back. It also accepts a **Mihon or Tachiyomi backup** (`.tachibk`), so you can bring your library from Android to your Suwayomi server.
- **Automated backup** creates backups on the server on a schedule; set the interval, time and folder, and how many to keep.

## Migration

When a source stops working, **Migrate** moves titles to another source. Choose what to carry over: chapters, categories, tracking, downloads and reader settings. You can migrate one series or many at once.
