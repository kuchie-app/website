## Local collection

Choose **Save to device** on a chapter. It's stored in the Local profile and opens without a connection.

## Server collection

There are two kinds of download:

- **Download to server** puts the chapter in the server's download queue.
- **On-device downloads** keep chapters on the iPhone for offline reading. Kuchie can top them up as you read.

Useful options in the offline settings:

- **Download over Wi-Fi only**
- **Simultaneous downloads**
- **Auto-remove old downloads** after a number of days
- **Keep recently-read chapters downloaded**

## Live Activities

While chapters download, progress shows in Live Activities and the Dynamic Island. Pick the style you like: the bookmark scarf with a progress ring, or Kiri's face with a percentage.

## Free up space

Remove downloads per series, or use **Remove all downloads**. Your server library isn't touched.
